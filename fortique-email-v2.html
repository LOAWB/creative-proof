What was actually wrong. The old set was a photo band with all the words sitting in the email underneath it. That is the thing you called little cutouts stitched and added, and at 1200 by 520 it lands as a 145 pixel strip on a phone, which is the mobile half of the same problem. So the two complaints were one cause.

What changed. The graphic is now the design: eyebrow, headline, product, and the button all inside one image, the way the examples you sent are built. Portrait, so it has presence on a phone. Nothing is set smaller than about 11 pixels as it lands on a phone screen, which is checked on every build rather than eyeballed.

Still open, and yours to decide. The welcome flow has no offer in it. Your examples all carry a first order discount and the brief says the welcome flow is usually built around one. I have not invented a percentage. Send a code and a number and it goes into the welcome graphic the same day.

One trade worth knowing. Copy inside an image cannot be read by a client that blocks images. Every graphic ships with written alt text that carries the same message, and the buttons and legal footer stay as live text in the email underneath.
