What changed from the last set

Their look, not just their angles. Light ground with the graph paper grid they use, centred heavy caps headline, wordmark and tagline at the top, the deliverable stamp at the bottom. Our editorial dark style is gone.

The vial is photographic. Generated on a transparent ground so one render drops onto any frame. Blank label, no text, no markings, no syringe, nothing that turns a B2B ad into a consumer peptide ad.

Velocity's own colours, as confirmed: the aqua and teal from their stylesheet rather than Launch Media's yellow.
