Worth knowing before these run

  • The review frame is empty on purpose. It is a finished layout with no quote and no name, and it says so on the frame. Send real reviews with permission and it fills the same day.
  • There are no performance numbers on any of these. The reference set prints 15X ROAS, 12.4X ROAS and 4 percent CVR. I have no real Velocity figures, so any number I put on a frame would be invented about your client's own results and your client is the one who wears it. Send the real ones and I will build a numbers variant the same day at no cost.
  • Product is unbranded on purpose. Blank white label, no text, no compound name, no measurement markings, no syringe and no fill line. That keeps a B2B ad from reading as a consumer peptide ad.
  • Their look, your colours. Composition, treatment and formats are theirs. The accent is Velocity's aqua and teal off their own stylesheet, and the type is their Fraunces and GeistSans rather than the Inter the earlier batches used.
  • The patient angle in their folder does not transfer. A chunk of that set sells to telehealth clinics who treat patients, and one set is a different brand. These all talk to the person who owns the brand.

Two things would make this set stronger and both are yours to send: real Velocity performance numbers, and real reviews with permission. Neither is invented here.
