Unzips to one folder. 1200 x 520 px each, which is 2x the 600px email width so they stay sharp on retina.
What changedSix of the eight drafts had no imagery in them at all. Their only image was Omnisend's own badge at the foot. The two that did have a photo used the square catalogue shot dropped in whole. These replace that with a composed band per email.

Two things worth knowingNo text is baked into any of these files, so the headline and copy stay editable in Omnisend and still read when a mail client blocks images. The only words visible are the ones printed on the vial label in the photograph.

Three shots in the shared Drive folder were not used: the vials labelled Retatrutide, Tirzepatide and Semaglutide. The shop sells those three as GLP-1, GLP-2 and GLP-3 and never prints the compound name anywhere on the site, so the store's own GLP artwork was used instead. Two others in that folder are the older strength, AOD-9604 and Sermorelin, which the shop now lists at 5mg.
