MRP TikTok set, round 01

Ten 9:16 video ads, 9 seconds each, in the one-vial-one-world style of the reference page, in MRP navy, blue and silver. Every ad has its own concept, vial count and pose, layout and original music. No end card.

01. Glacier Cave

One vial upright on an ice ledge inside a glowing blue glacier cave, light shafts; big top headline.

02. Deep Trio

Three vials hanging at different depths in deep blue open water under sun rays; left-aligned headline.

03. Salt Mirror

A vial lying on its side on a blue-hour mirror salt flat rises upright on its own reflection; headline hands over to the CTA.

04. Aurora Pair

Two vials, near and far, on a dark basalt ridge under a rippling blue aurora; staggered left/right headline.

05. Waterfall Perch

One vial tilted on wet black rock before a misty blue waterfall ($0 local 2.5D: falling water, mist, spray, label glint); icon row + stacked tagline.

06. Ice Overhead

Top-down: one vial lying diagonally on cracked blue lake ice; text sits in a frosted navy glass card.

07. Storm Stack

Low-angle hero: one big vial on a sea stack above crashing navy waves; giant stacked words.

08. Stratosphere

One vial floating tilted high above the blue curve of the Earth at sunrise; centered headline with a drawn rule.

09. Lagoon Pair

Two vials half-sunk in a milky blue geothermal lagoon with drifting steam; right-aligned shipping copy.

10. Moon Dunes

A small vial alone on a moonlit dune crest, sand veils blowing, slow push-in; copy up in the empty starry sky.
