Axion TikTok set, round 01

Ten 9:16 video ads, 9 seconds each, in Axion's own indigo, royal blue and periwinkle, set in Poppins like the site. Every ad makes a different argument in its own world, with its own layout and original music, and closes on the Axion lockup with VIEW COA. The vial is the real Axion blank-label artwork in every frame.

01 Cryo Chrome

Proof

Cryogenic vapour rolls across polished chrome around one upright vial; crisp indigo slab headline.

02 City Ledge

Traceability

One vial on a wet black granite ledge high above a blue-hour city, fine drizzle; copy in a right-hand rail.

03 Cleanroom Trio

Consistency

Clean room: three vials receding in a diagonal line on white acrylic under cyan rim light; dark ink on the light scene.

04 Star Plinth

Identity

One vial on a black obsidian plinth while a four-point star of light blooms behind it (the Axion mark's star); centred type.

05 Prism Bench

Method

One vial lying on its side on a dark optical bench while thin laser lines split through glass prisms behind; lab-note chyron.

06 Wind Tunnel

Question

A big tilted vial in a dark wind tunnel while white smoke streamlines flow past it; the question flips to the answer.

07 Hex Twin

Quiz

Two identical vials side by side on softly pulsing hexagonal glass tiles; a three-answer quiz where BOTH lights up.

08 Vitrine

Crossed

One small vial on a white pedestal inside a museum glass vitrine, cool spotlight, dust motes; 'Trust us' gets struck to 'Check us'.

09 Frost Macro

Engineered

Macro: a huge vial angled and bled off the left edge on cold dark glass beaded with condensation; one-word kinetic type.

10 Ink Tank

Negative

One vial in front of a glass tank where indigo and violet ink clouds bloom slowly; a negative hook reverses to 'Read the report.'
