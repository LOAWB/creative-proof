clip 01 · $0.80

Seeded from the square master

letterboxedno reveallabel drift

Unusable. The picture is a 720×720 square floating in a 720×1280 frame, so 44% of it is black bars. Cause: it was seeded from the square product shot and the model kept that shape instead of filling the tall frame. The vapour reveal also never happens, because when you seed from a still, that still is the first frame, so it cannot open on a hidden product. Around three seconds the left vial's blue band washes out.

clip 02 · $0.80 · after a $0.20 vertical master

Seeded from a vertical master

frame filledlabel holds at both endsmid-clip occlusion

The framing problem is gone: the picture now fills 720×1272 of the 720×1280 frame. The logo is correct and readable at the start and the end. What breaks is the middle: around four seconds a blurred flask sweeps across and covers the wordmark, and the model invents two extra vials behind. That is my brief's fault, not the tool's. It asked for foreground glassware to travel between the camera and the product, which is an open invitation to cover the label.

What the two clips actually bought

  • A vertical master fixes framing. Confirmed by measurement, not by eye: content area went from 720×720 to 720×1272.
  • Seeding means the product exists in frame one. Any concept where the product arrives, is revealed, or drops into shot is impossible forward. Those get rendered backwards and the file flipped, which costs nothing extra.
  • Foreground elements have to stay at the frame edges. The rule is now written into every motion prompt: nothing crosses in front of the product, and the vial count can never change.
