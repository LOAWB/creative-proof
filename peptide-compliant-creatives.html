The Compliant Peptide Creative Playbook

How to make peptide ad creatives that actually pass Meta review, and why the most compliant angle is also the strongest one in the category.

The one idea that runs through all of this: for peptides, you win by selling trust and verification, not health outcomes. Meta reviews peptide ads harder than almost any category. The moment a creative promises a result, shows a dose on a vial, or implies you put it in your body, it is a rejection risk. Shift the whole message to quality you can verify and the same creative sails through review, and it happens to be the single biggest differentiator a serious peptide brand has.

Why peptide ads get killed

These are the landmines. Every one of them is avoidable without weakening the ad.

Avoid Claim and imagery landmines

  • Dose on the vial. A label reading a compound plus a milligram amount is a documented rejection shape. Keep vials clean or brand-only in ad art.
  • Health, medical or therapeutic claims. Words like heal, treat, cure, recover, injury, inflammation, testosterone, fat loss, muscle gain.
  • Before and after body transformation. The classic ecom format is high risk the second the body is the proof.
  • Needles or injection imagery, or anything implying human ingestion or use.
  • Naming a specific research compound next to an implied human benefit.

Use instead The compliant claim toolkit

  • Sell the proof, not the promise. Third party lab tested. COA on every product. Purity you can check yourself.
  • Substantiate with lab data, not health results. A purity percentage or a testing pass rate is a fact about the product, not a claim about a body.
  • Soften any remaining claim: "look of", "up to", "designed for", with an asterisk to the source.
  • Trust furniture: money back guarantee, made in the USA, ships from the USA, real reviews about service and transparency.
  • Verification as the hero: the QR on the vial, the COA download, the batch number.

How to brief every peptide creative

1 Lead the hook with trust or verification, not a result.

2 Any number on the creative is a lab or service fact (purity, pass rate, ship time), asterisked to a source.

3 Vials are clean or brand-only. No compound plus dose on the label.

4 No body as proof, no needles, no implied human use.

5 Add trust furniture: COA, third party tested, US based, guarantee, real reviews about service.

6 Run it through a pre-flight check for claim words before it ever reaches ad review.
