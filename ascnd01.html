Three things worth a look before these run

Each one is a place where the guide's example and your live site disagreed, and the site won. Say the word on any of them and it is a quick change.

No "third-party tested" badge anywhere. The guide's example ads carry one. Your COA library says all 22 published reports were supplier supplied and none were commissioned by ASCND Research, and the site is careful never to present a supplier's report as your own testing. The badge would claim exactly what you avoid claiming, so the chips talk about documentation instead.

The sourcing angle is about the paperwork, not the factory. The guide's version sells documented U.S.-made production. The site says orders ship from within the United States, which is fulfilment rather than manufacture, so that ad asks who ran the test and who commissioned it. If domestic production is true and you want it said, it needs to come from you in writing.

No prices and no thresholds on the artwork. Flat-rate shipping and free over a threshold are both real and both on the site, but the guide bans offer language and any percentage anywhere, so fulfilment is sold as predictability and tracking instead.
