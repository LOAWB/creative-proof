Axion Research, email flows

Every page below is read back out of the live Omnisend account and rendered from what is actually stored, at desktop and phone width. Not mock ups.

All five are DRAFTS and switched OFF. Nothing has been sent.
Welcome
3 emails, 2 days apart, starting immediately on subscribe. Carries the 10% per recipient code.
Abandoned cart
2 emails, 30 minutes after the cart goes quiet, then 22 hours. Exits on purchase.
Abandoned checkout
2 emails, 1 hour after checkout stalls, then 22 hours. Exits on purchase.
Browse abandonment
1 email only, 30 minutes after a product view. Stands down if they cart the item.
Replenishment
1 email, 30 days after an order. Exits if they have already reordered.
No abandonment email carries a discount. The only offer in the whole set is the welcome one, and that code is generated per recipient by the store at send time, so nothing depends on a coupon existing by hand.
