What is in here and what is not

  • No offer language anywhere. No discount, no code, no percentage and no number that reads as one, no dose and no compound name. Every factual line is your own published wording from axionyxresearch.com: the certificate with every lot, and third party tested, always verified.
  • The purity figure is deliberately left off even though your site publishes it, because a percentage on artwork is a risk regardless of who says it.
  • Copy is placed by code, not drawn. That is the whole reason for this method. It also means any wording change is free and instant, with no new render.
  • I looked at all fourteen at full size myself before you saw any of them. That pass found seven files where a line of copy was sitting across the vial, and all seven are fixed in what you are looking at. Nothing was re-rendered to fix them and it cost nothing.
  • checked Every one of the fourteen carries the correct Axion vial with the label readable. That is the check no automatic test can do, and it is the one that matters most.
