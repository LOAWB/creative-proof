What changed in this round

You asked for the blur off this batch and said you would check it for misalignments. Both below, including one I found doing that check and the part of the blur I cannot fix for free.

A focus pass across all ten. It lifts definition back up wherever the image actually holds detail, and it is free, no re-rendering. You can see it most on the linen weave, the travertine and the ceramic tray. The originals are untouched on my side, so nothing here is destructive.

What the blur actually is, because it is not uniform. The vials and the labels were never soft. At full resolution the wordmark, the glass edges and the stone pores all resolve cleanly. What is soft is the BACKGROUND on three of them: the window and the plant behind the desk shot, the dried grass on the limestone, and the far fold of the linen. That is depth of field in the original renders.

What the pass cannot do. Sharpening lifts detail that is slightly soft. It cannot invent detail the lens never caught, so the real background blur on those three is still there and will stay there. Removing it properly means re-rendering those scenes with a deep-focus rule, which spends on your key. Say the word and I will, and I would rather ask than spend it on a guess. Worth saying plainly: on the other seven what reads as blur is mostly an empty surface, a plain wall or a seamless sweep with nothing in it to be sharp, and no amount of sharpening changes that.

The misalignment you would have found. On ad 8, the option pills one, the VIEW THE CATALOG button was running off the LEFT edge of the frame on both sizes. Not close to it, exactly on it, so the rounded left end of the button was sliced flat. The cause was that one layout giving its footer row no side margin while every other layout does. It now sits inside the same margin as the copy above it.

And a check so it is not eyes next time. Every element is now measured against the frame edge on every build, in the real renderer. It proves itself each run by planting a deliberate overflow and refusing to report a pass unless it catches it. Against the old code it reports that button at 0.0px and fails; against this build all twenty files are clear. I also measured every ad before and after: the only geometry that moved in the whole set is that button, so nothing else shifted to pay for the fix.
