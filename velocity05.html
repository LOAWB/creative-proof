Where the angles came from

The reference folder turned out to be far bigger than it first looked: 101 assets across nine weekly folders, not the nine files sitting in the root. Those nine were the flattest of the set. The weekly folders are named by angle, so nine weeks of naming shows what the account kept coming back to, and repeated means it earned another week of spend. That recurrence is what picked these ten.

It also settled a question that had been open. The root nine are mostly a centred block of type on a flat colour field. The later weeks are not: rows of vials receding into darkness, a helix built out of vials, a lab interior at shallow depth of field, a news bar over a real photograph. They moved away from the flat look on their own. So using their angles and building at a high standard were never actually in conflict.

Brand, taken from the site rather than guessed

Every colour here is one of Velocity's own --color-* tokens, read straight from their live stylesheet: aqua bright #4fe0d2, teal deep #093b3f, ink #0f1b1b, bone #f1f4f4. Earlier batches had to derive a palette from screenshots of their ads, which got close but was not theirs.

Reading the site also turned up something four batches had missed. Their display face is Fraunces, a serif, with GeistSans for text. Everything before this was set in Inter, so none of it was actually in the client's type. Both faces are served by their own site and both are open licensed. That is the single biggest change in how this batch reads.

Two things held back, and why

No performance numbers. Income framing is cleared and it is the client's call to make. Inventing the figures is a different thing: their reference set prints 15X ROAS, 12.4X ROAS, 4% CVR and a spend dashboard, and nobody has given us a single real Velocity number. Putting one on these would be a false statement about the client's own results, and the client is who carries it. So this batch uses scale and outcome language freely and asserts no metric. Send real numbers and a numbers variant is a free re-render, same day.

No testimonials. Their set carries named five star reviews from seven different people across several weeks. If Velocity has real ones with permission to use them, that is a strong angle and it gets built properly with real attribution.

Worth knowing about the folder itself: it is not one campaign. Some of it sells to telehealth clinics who treat patients rather than to brand owners, and one set belongs to a different brand called Telescale. The clinic framing is the part that does not transfer, so angle 05 is that split rewritten for someone who owns the business.
